📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data and provide actionable insights into workforce structure, employee status, promotions, retrenchment, satisfaction, and job roles.

🎯 Project Overview

This project provides an interactive view of the organization's workforce through 3 dashboard pages:

- 🏠 Home
- ⚡ Action
- 📋 Details

The dashboard uses interactive navigation buttons to make it easy to move between pages and explore different HR insights.

---

🏠 1. Home

The Home page provides a high-level overview of the workforce.

Key KPIs

- Total Employees: 1.47K
- Male: 882 (60%)
- Female: 588 (40%)
- On Service: 1.353K (92%)
- Retrench: 117 (8%)
- Due for Promotion: 72 (4.9%)

Key Visualizations

- Service Years
- Job Level
- Employee Distance
- Promotion Status
- Retrenchment Status

This page gives HR teams a quick overview of the organization's workforce.

---

⚡ 2. Action

The Action page focuses on HR decisions related to employee promotion and retrenchment.

Key Insights

- Employees due for promotion
- Employees due for retrenchment
- Promotion and retrenchment by job role
- Employee satisfaction levels
- Overtime distribution
- Total employees by job role

Business Purpose

This page helps HR teams identify areas that may require action and supports workforce planning and decision-making.

---

📋 3. Details

The Details page provides a more detailed view of employee information.

It allows users to explore employee-level data and analyze workforce characteristics in greater detail.

Detailed Analysis

- Employee information
- Job roles
- Department
- Job level
- Years of service
- Satisfaction
- Overtime
- Promotion status
- Retrenchment status

This page is designed for users who need to move from high-level KPIs to more detailed employee analysis.

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How many employees are currently working?
- What is the gender distribution?
- How many employees are due for promotion?
- How many employees are due for retrenchment?
- Which job roles have the highest number of employees?
- What is the employee satisfaction distribution?
- How does overtime vary among employees?
- What are the different employee service years and job levels?
